    How to play

    Hits raise the other potato's damage, and the higher it is, the farther they fly. Knock them off the screen to take a life.

    More tips
    Combos
    Light hits chain. The third light hit in a row knocks the opponent away. After the second, a Heavy, Special or Jump comes out faster: that's the link.
    Recover
    Knocked off the side? Jump in the air, then up + Heavy to fly back. Fall near the edge to grab it.
    Steer
    Hold the stick while you're flying from a hit to bend your path. Holding up keeps you on screen longer.
    Breakfall
    Press Dodge just before you hit the ground after a big hit: no bounce, quick recovery.
    Platforms
    Hold down on a platform to drop through it.
    Gamepad
    A connected gamepad also works where the browser allows it.
